## 2. Where docs/REUSE_MAP.md or docs/ROADMAP.md is contradicted or cannot be confirmed

Contradicted:
1. REUSE_MAP row "Claim-level graph checking: FreeGuard ... NOT FOUND ... do not cite until located". It exists: Wang and Zhao, Discover Computing, 7 Oct 2026 (section 1). The "do not cite" instruction can be lifted. The "related: Evidence Graph Consistency (2606.06748)" is not checked here.
2. ROADMAP item 5 and REUSE_MAP rely on UniCR (2509.01455) as a shift-robust guarantee. The paper is withdrawn on arXiv (reason on the page: unverifiable authorship and affiliation). It should be dropped as supporting evidence. Its abstract also states no exchangeability condition.
3. ROADMAP item 7 attributes to "GRACE and GRAB-RAG" the findings that misleading or conflicting evidence is the main failure and that an NLI verifier fails when parametric memory and the wrong passage agree. Per the abstracts, those findings belong to GRAB-RAG only (small frozen models, 3.8B to 8B, Natural Questions and HotpotQA, passages edited by the authors). The GRACE abstract is about RL training for sufficiency judgement and answer/abstain, and does not mention misleading evidence. The phrase "abstention on missing evidence is manageable" is supported by GRAB-RAG for prompted small models, not by GRACE.
4. REUSE_MAP table says TIAR 2605.25850 and AWA-RL 2607.10738 are "not checked" and groups them with "I-CALM (prompt-only)". They are now checked (section 1); I-CALM was not examined. TIAR is an RL method (adaptive abstention reward in GRPO on AbstentionBench), not prompt-only; AWA-RL is a search-agent RL method with stated public code/weights.

Cannot be confirmed from the pages I read:
5. ROADMAP item 3: "increase hallucination roughly linearly; filtering/early stopping helps" for Gekhman et al. The page text I received says examples are learned slowly and then increase hallucination, and says nothing I could see about linearity, filtering or early stopping. These may be in the paper body or the original abstract wording; not confirmed here. The "EMNLP 2024" label is confirmed.
6. ROADMAP S4 and item 5: "ITCR needs exchangeable calibration/test data" (REUSE_MAP). The ITCR abstract does not state exchangeability; it is the usual conformal assumption but not confirmed for this paper. Same for Mohri and Hashimoto and for Cherian and Gibbs (abstracts silent). Only SCoRE states exchangeability explicitly.
7. ROADMAP S4 lists SCoRE (2603.24704) together with Mohri-Hashimoto and Cherian-Gibbs as "conformal risk control". Per the abstracts these are different tools: Mohri-Hashimoto and Cherian-Gibbs filter or back off claims with split or conditional conformal calibration; SCoRE uses e-values to select trusted cases with bounded-risk control; ITCR stops generation in reasoning graphs. None is stated to be an answer-versus-abstain procedure for short QA; S4 will need to define the score and the risk explicitly.
8. REUSE_MAP: Abstain-R1 "ACL 2026 Findings". The page says only "accepted at ACL 2026"; "Findings" is not confirmed. Weights location (huggingface.co/leoleung04/Abstain-R1) and absence of code were not re-checked.
9. REUSE_MAP: Mohri-Hashimoto and Cherian-Gibbs venues are not stated in the notes; the pages I got did not show venues either, so none is asserted here.
10. REUSE_MAP / ROADMAP facts about papers not in my task list (Kalai et al. Nature 2026, FaithEval, AbstentionBench, SimpleQA Verified, VISTA, Science One, R-Tuning, 2601.20126, 2510.17733, 2603.21172, 2606.02628, 2404.05405, 2509.04664, 2606.06748) were not re-checked.

Incidental: arXiv 2608.22228's reference list (seen only via a search snippet) cites the "Sufficient Context" paper as 2601.14277. That id is a llama.cpp quantization paper (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.14277, opened). The Sufficient Context paper is arXiv 2411.06037 (section 3).
