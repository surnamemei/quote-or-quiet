### Model outputs to be achieved (what the user sees from their own model)
The user's daily models are Flash Next and the 27B NVFP4 on Infernix. The end state is defined on the answer the user reads, not on the tool's JSON.
- O1, public facts. Every factual statement in the visible answer is either (a) Verified: the answer, a verbatim quote that states it, the page and its hash; or (b) explicitly marked as not verified. No unverified candidate is stated as fact.
- O2, material the user gives (files, pasted text). The answer comes from the material; if the material does not state it the answer says so; if records conflict it gives each; a value from the model's memory is never shown as if the material said it.
- O3, it runs on the user's setup: one model loaded at a time in Infernix, with the desktop's memory share left free.

## 2. What the recheck found
1. The tool that passed G15 is not what the MCP server serves. tool/qoq_mcp.py still calls the version 1 routes (/answer, /bio); the validated version 2 routes (/answer2, /check, /bio2) are reachable only over HTTP. tool/README.md still describes version 1 (Phi-4, all-sources agreement).
2. No end-to-end test exists on the visible answer of a host model. The tool returns "Unverified candidate, do not state as fact: X"; whether a host model then repeats X as fact has never been measured. O1 is therefore untested.
3. The judge is the Ollama 27B (about 20 GB). Flash Next fills the GPU and runs alone, so the present tool cannot sit beside the user's daily model. O3 needs the judge to run in the user's runtime: the 27B NVFP4, or Flash Next itself. Neither has been validated as a judge (decoys, human-label check), and the two 12B models that were tried as reviewers failed the decoys, so this cannot be assumed.
4. Track C has a diagnosis and no guard. The verbatim check against the given material (planned as the first step of Track C) was never built; O2 is untested.
5. Paper: the abstract and conclusion stop at S57 to S63; they do not mention the check behind other models, the baselines, the stress test, the NQ-open result, the human-label check or Track C. No figure shows the gates.
6. docs/PLAN_TO_FULL_BAR.md still says 15 of 48; its G4 clause ("if coverage stays under 35% the gate is reported as not reachable and the objective is restricted") now applies.
7. The bar cannot be filled by compute: of the 7 missing criteria, 6 need people (G12, G16) and 1 is G4.

## 6. Revision after two outside reviews (2026-11-13, same day)
Two reviews written by another assistant and passed on by the user were checked against the project's files. Claims about papers in them (CopyPasteLLM, RHIO, Fakepedia) are not taken as given and are checked at the source before any use; GopherCite and Sufficient Context are already in the related work.

Adopted, with the reason:
1. Measurement first (new step 0, before step 1). The reviews showed by construction that the C1 scorer marks 'X, not Y' as the model's own value and ignores additions. Checked on the stored answers: it changed the headline (K6 above; docs/TRACK_C_CONTEXT_FAITHFUL_PLAN.md, correction). Step 0: (a) score the primary answer and its mentions apart (done for B and C); (b) semantic review of a random sample and of all disagreeing cases for additions, negation, attribution and conflict answers that pick one, with decoys; (c) a no-material control for every item, so that 'the model knows another value' is measured and not assumed; (d) drop constructed items that are not valid (a replaced value that can also be true; two values that do not conflict, such as two occupations); (e) a small set of real-style material (a report, a long conversation), since the C1 sentences were selected by an earlier check and are not ordinary input.
2. 'Not found' is not 'not stated'. The /context route returns one of: supported (quote); conflicting (each quote); not found in the checked text, with what was checked; whole material checked and no statement found. The visible wording follows the state.
3. Two modes, tested apart: extraction (the answer is a span of the material; mechanical check) and evidence-based inference (unit conversion, translation, comparison, a calculation; the inputs and the steps are shown and the inputs are checked). Step 1 builds extraction only and says so.
4. Usefulness gets a threshold. D3 passes only if, besides its error bounds, correct completion of answerable items is at least 90% and not more than 5 points below the instruction-only baseline. D4 keeps latency as reported, measured from question to usable answer including model switches.
5. K1 is worded as what was tested (above); the paper's 'exact' statement is kept only for the property the code enforces on every release path, after the path audit.
6. The VitaminC figure is a validity warning, not a correction factor; the k/0.95 sentence is removed from the paper and the S71 record.
7. The model line is not conditional on the guard failing (decision above). Its design: original model; instruction; one published context-faithfulness training method, verified at source; a candidate built from the model's own real errors. Measured together: unsupported answers, completion of answerable items, guessing on missing facts, needless refusal, general ability, and whether the gain survives conversion back into the user's runtime. Trained first on the 4B to prove the pipeline; a 4B result is not read as a 27B result. Flash Next stays evaluation-only.
8. The closing comparison of the whole programme is the two-by-two table: original or improved model, without or with the guard.
9. No new encyclopedia gates. The 48-criterion bar is kept as the record of the public-fact work and is not extended.

Qualified:
- The reviews call the C1 changed-value finding real ('reproduced on your models'). After the audit it is not: the models follow changed material. The model line's targets are therefore guessing on missing facts and needless refusal, and the size of both is modest once an instruction is given (2.5% and up to 13.5%). If step 0 confirms that, the case for training rests on needless refusal and on real-style material, and may turn out weak; that would be reported as the result.
- Novelty: agreed that quote-plus-abstain, sufficiency and faithfulness training exist. The paper claims measurements and a tested rule, as its related-work section already says; no claim of a new method is added.

Order now: step 0 (measurement; CPU plus reviewers), step 1 (tool), step 2 (judge on the user's models), step 3 (end-to-end tests), model line in parallel from the end of step 0 as GPU time allows, step 4 (close).
