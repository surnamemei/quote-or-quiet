# S64: gate G13, the quote check against standard checkers (plan, 2026-11-11)

Gate G13: the quote rule has a lower share of not-stating quotes than every baseline at equal acceptance; at least 900 held-out rows; registered on data not used to design any checker.
Baselines: MiniCheck-Flan-T5-Large (S54) and HHEM-2.1-Open (Vectara; downloaded 2026-11-11 with the user's permission; its custom code was read and not run, the model rebuilt from the flan-t5-base config and the published weights). AlignScore is not run: its checkpoint is a pickle file, which can execute code on loading.
Exploratory (post hoc, before this plan; results/runs/s64/HHEM_result.md): on the 939 held-out rows of S51 the 27B judge accepted 716 with 1.1% not 'states'; at the same acceptance MiniCheck 3.1% and HHEM 4.3%. On the 361 Mintaka rows: 27B 162 accepted, 1.9% misleading; MiniCheck 7.4%, HHEM 9.9%.
Confirmatory test (registered now, before HHEM or MiniCheck has been run on these rows): the 300 displayed quotes (first accepted span, chosen by no judge) of the S40 quote analysis, with their blind two-reviewer labels (misleading = both 'not'; 'not states' = not both 'states'). Checkers: the 27B prompt judge (YES to the rule R prompt, from the S40 run; a quote that failed the sentence or number check counts as not accepted), MiniCheck and HHEM at the same number accepted.
Prediction: among the accepted quotes, the share 'not states' is lower for the 27B than for MiniCheck and for HHEM; misleading likewise (both may be small). N criterion of the gate (900) is met by the exploratory held-out set, not by this set (300); the confirmatory set tests the ordering on data that no checker has seen.

## Result (2026-11-11 15:4x; results/runs/s64/G13_confirm_result.md, HHEM_result.md)

Confirmatory, 300 displayed S40 quotes (40 not 'states', 34 misleading among all): the 27B judge accepted 236 with 1 not 'states' (0.4%, upper 2.3%) and 1 misleading; at the same acceptance MiniCheck had 7 not 'states' (3.0%, upper 6.0%) and 4 misleading, HHEM 5 (2.1%, upper 4.9%) and 2. The prediction (27B lowest on both measures) held.
Gate G13: T met (lower than both baselines at equal acceptance on the held-out, Mintaka and confirmatory sets), N met (939 held-out rows), V met (confirmatory comparison registered before the baselines were run on those rows). The gate passes. Limits: AlignScore not run (pickle checkpoint); on the confirmatory set the differences are a few quotes and were not tested for significance; the 27B is a much larger model than the two baselines (0.25B and 0.8B), so the comparison is of checkers as used, not of methods at equal size; reviewers are language models.
