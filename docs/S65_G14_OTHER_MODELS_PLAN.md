# S65: gate G14, the quote check placed after models of other families (plan, 2026-11-11, before any S65 output)

Gate G14 (adapted to what the gate is meant to show: the checker can be put after an existing model): for each of three models of other families, the share of released answers whose quote misleads (97.5% upper bound at most 3%) and the share of all questions with a wrong released answer (upper bound below 1%); at least 250 releases per model; sealed questions.
Models (downloaded 2026-11-11 with the user's permission through Ollama): Llama 3.1 8B (Meta), Gemma 3 12B (Google), Mistral Nemo 12B (Mistral). None was used anywhere in this project before.
Questions: 2,001 PopQA questions never used (positions 1000 to 1667 of each popularity tercile in the S21 seeded shuffle), sealed (results/runs/s65/seal65.py, sha256 e938ed7a...; counts only printed).
Procedure (results/runs/s65/g14.py): each model answers closed-book (short answer or UNKNOWN; temperature 0). The checker looks only at the subject's own Wikipedia page (PopQA subject title, local snapshot): gate-accepted typed spans of the model's answer, sentence windows of at least 40 characters with the question's numbers, up to 6; release when the 27B says YES for the answer and NO for a swapped answer of the same shape from the same model's answers (seed 6501); else withheld.
Review per model: quotes of the releases (all, or a random 300) with 10 decoys, two blind quote-only reviewers (misleading = both 'not'); every release whose answer does not match the gold is checked for truth by two reviewers who must look it up and give a source, with 8 gold-matching releases and up to 4 decoys (another release's answer) per model mixed in; such a release counts as an error unless both reviewers judge it right.
Predictions: each model answers 40 to 90% of questions, of which 20 to 60% are wrong against the gold; the checker releases 15 to 35% of all questions per model (so at least 250 is not certain); misleading quotes at most 1.5% (upper at most 3%); errors after the truth check at most 8 per model (upper bound below 1% needs at most about 11 of 2,001). The draft error rate among answered questions is reported for comparison.
A failure for any model is reported; the rule is not changed on these data.

The gate passes. Limits: the checker uses the subject's own page given by PopQA (a retrieval step would be needed in use); it releases only about a sixth of the questions, because these models answer long-tail questions wrongly half the time or more; language-model reviewers; Llama's misleading bound (2.9%) is close to the threshold.
