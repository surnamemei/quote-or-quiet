# S68: gate G4, second test: rule Q2 on 1,500 sealed NQ-open questions (plan, 2026-11-12, before any S68 run output)

Why. The first G4 test (S58, Mintaka) answered 35.2% but left 7 adjudicated errors of 1,200 (upper bound 1.20%): 4 were answers both adjudicators judged right (equivalent to the gold) that the registered rule did not let them clear, 2 failed only because the quote matcher did not read template-written text, 1 was an out-of-date count. The rule Q2 is not changed; the adjudication is fixed in advance on those two points, and new questions are used because Mintaka's generic questions are used up.
Questions: 1,500 NQ-open validation questions (natural Google queries with short answers; google-research-datasets/nq_open, CC BY-SA 3.0; downloaded 2026-11-12), drawn with seed 6801 and sealed (results/runs/s68/seal68.py, sha256 144fd0fb...; counts only printed). None used before.
Pipeline: the frozen S19 pipeline up to the six readers (free-search-mcp pool, 10 to 12 s between queries; 4B server cap 68%, generation batch budget 12,000 tokens as in the finished S58 run); a passage over 60,000 characters is not read (as found in S58); then rule Q2 exactly as registered in docs/S58_G4_PLAN.md (seed 6802 for swaps).
Adjudication (fixed now): two adjudicators, gold shown, look up every gold-wrong release; verdicts answer right / equally acceptable / gold wrong / answer wrong / cannot tell. A release is cleared when both give 'answer right' (equivalent to the gold), or both give a removing verdict (equally acceptable or gold wrong) with a supporting quote that the mechanical check finds in the named Wikipedia page (local snapshot, else the current wikitext or rendered text). Otherwise it is an error. 12 decoys and 24 gold-matching releases mixed in; valid when at least 11 of 12 decoys are kept as wrong and at most 2 of 24 correct releases are called wrong by both.
Gate G4 T: coverage at least 35% and the 97.5% upper bound of errors/1,500 below 1% (at most 7 errors). N: 1,500. V: as above.
Predictions: coverage 25 to 40% (a real chance of missing 35% on these questions); gold-wrong releases 1 to 4% of questions; adjudicated errors 2 to 8; probability of passing about one half.

## Result, part 1 (2026-11-13 01:05; before adjudication)
Rule Q2 released 256 of 1,500 sealed NQ-open questions: coverage 17.1%. Withheld: no witness 1,166, reader support 1 only 45, time-sensitive 18, conflicting witnesses 15. Against the gold 30 releases were wrong (2.00% of questions; 11.7% of releases).
Against the predictions: coverage 25 to 40%: FAILED (17.1%). Gold-wrong 1 to 4% of questions: met (2.00%). Adjudicated errors 2 to 8: pending.
Gate G4: coverage at least 35% NOT met, so T fails whatever the adjudication finds. The gate fails its second test (T not met; N met, 1,500; V met).
Where coverage was lost (counts only): of the 1,166 'no witness' questions, 185 had no candidate (readers disagree or abstain), 715 had candidates but no gate-accepted typed span on any retrieved Wikipedia page, and 266 had spans that the 27B judge did not accept. 1,050 questions had a candidate with support of at least 2. The typed-span checks (V1 to V5) were built for short Wikidata-typed answers; NQ-open answers are often free phrases ('economic recession', 'by mid-1988'), which they rarely accept. The adjudication runs as registered (two adjudicators, A and B, working alone) to report the error count; it cannot change the gate.

## Result, part 2: adjudication (2026-11-13, 01:2x; results/runs/s68/G4_result.md)
Two adjudicators (A, B; separate Claude agents, same instructions, each blind to the other: their transcripts show that neither opened the other's file) looked up all 66 sheet rows. The mechanical check found the supporting quote for 26 of 28 removing verdicts of A and 27 of 28 of B. Decoys kept as wrong by both: 12 of 12; gold-matching releases called wrong by both: 0 of 24 (valid).
Adjudicated errors: 5 of 1,500 (0.33% of questions; 97.5% upper bound 0.78%); 5 of 256 releases (2.0%). Against the prediction 2 to 8: met.
Gate G4: upper bound below 1% met; coverage at least 35% NOT met (17.1%). The gate fails its second test on coverage.
Note on the adjudicators: A and B gave the same verdict on all 66 rows and the same free-text note on 35, although neither saw the other's file. Two agents of the same model with the same instructions are close to deterministic; they are not two independent judgements, and the agreement says nothing about their accuracy. This is the reason gate G12 (human agreement) exists; it stays open.
Reading: on natural Google questions rule Q2 keeps its error rate low (5 errors in 1,500) but answers only one question in six; the loss is the typed-span step, not the judge. A fix must widen what counts as an answer span for free-phrase answers without opening the door to the errors the typed checks keep out; it would need a new design on design data and a third sealed test.
